# Compliance, Privacy & Quality Guardrails

1. **Claim only what works now**. Verify features on a fresh student test account before promotional claims. Pricing and trial offers require written founder approval.
2. **Never promise jobs, internships, placements, certificates, outcomes, discounts or mentors not verified as active.** No fabricated testimonials or client logos.
3. **Respect consent**. No bought lists, private-contact scraping, unsolicited bulk DM campaigns, forced group additions or spam automation.
4. **Follow-up limit:** maximum 2 for unanswered outreach, and immediately stop on an opt-out. For groups obtain administrator permission first.
5. **PUBLIC GitHub = public information:** role names, task checklists, approved content links and aggregated performance. Do not publish lead names, contact numbers, student account information, raw private feedback, screenshots of private chats, credentials or OTPs. Keep those records in a restricted private tracker.
6. **One contact owner**. Check the private tracker to avoid duplicate outreach and respect platform rules.
7. **Conversion evidence:** only count a connected student after genuine interest + registration + account access + completed initial onboarding. Verify privately.
8. **No fake metrics.** A day with zero outreach or zero registration is reported as zero, with explanation if blocked.
9. **Founder approval gates:** Day 1–2 features, registration test, links, copy, price/trial; before each publish; before any campus session promises; before final KPI claims.
10. **Never upload source PDFs to this public repo without a separate explicit publication check.** This repository is a newly drafted operations package, not the company's complete internal files.
