## Outcome definitions (do not confuse stages)

1. **Interested lead** = consented student who expresses genuine interest. Does NOT count as paid.
2. **Registration** = sign-up completed and account can be accessed. Still NOT paid.
3. **Paid student** = unique real student with **successfully settled/confirmed payment** in the authorised gateway/accounting records, a valid registration, working account access and initial onboarding completed. Record payment status and verification date privately. Exclude pending/failed/unverified, refunded or reversed payments from **net active paid total**. Never publish transaction IDs or customer information here.
4. **Business approached** = one *real, relevant business prospect* for an approved Heal Bharat Services offer, identified and qualified by public evidence and sent a personalised first outreach via an appropriate public business contact route, with date/channel/status privately logged. A one-way contact is **approached**, not **replied**, **meeting booked**, **client won** or **paying client**. Counting a paying business client would require agreed payment received, which is **not** this requested 15-day minimum.
5. **Business conversation** = a two-way response with an actual prospective decision-maker or designated representative. **Stretch target:** at least one response or discovery discussion, not guaranteed.

## Founder approval gate BEFORE public promotion

- Verify live CareerPluse registration, login, onboarding and legitimate checkout/payment flow. Test with approved methods; do not ask students to send OTP, password, card details or payment screenshots.
- Record confirmed actual price, taxes, refund policy, trial eligibility and payment processor in a **private approved claims register**. ₹999 and 7-day trial were discussed earlier as ideas, NOT assumed to be presently active. Never advertise until the founder confirms in writing and the live flow matches.
- Confirm which Heal Bharat Services offerings the company can really deliver, appropriate business pricing/quotation process and public official channels. No guarantees, fabricated outcomes or fake testimonials.
- If payments or signup are broken, pause paid-acquisition claims and escalate; work on education and trust until fixed.
